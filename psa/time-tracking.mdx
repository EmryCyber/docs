title: "Time Tracking & Billing"
description: "Track time with a live timer or the Log Time modal, submit and approve weekly timesheets, and turn approved time into invoices."
---

Regentra's time tracking ties technician work directly to tickets and projects, and feeds straight into invoicing. Every time entry moves through a submit/approve workflow — including a rejection path with resubmission — before it can be billed to a client.

## Logging time

Time can be logged two ways: a live timer on the ticket detail page, or the **Log Time** modal (also reachable from **Timesheets**).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Timer">
    On a ticket's detail page, click **Start** to begin timing your work. The elapsed time is shown live. Click **Stop** when you're done, and the Log Time modal opens pre-filled with the real start and end time the timer captured — review and save. Only one timer can run at a time; starting a new one prompts you to stop the one already running elsewhere.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Log Time modal">
    Click **Log Time** to open the entry form directly. Choose what you're logging against — a ticket (search by title or number) or a project task — enter a description, and set the duration.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Each time entry includes:

- **Log against** — a ticket or a project/task. The company is shown read-only, derived from whichever you pick.
- **Description** — what was done during this time.
- **Duration** — enter minutes directly, or switch to start/end time and let the modal compute the duration. Quick-add buttons (+15m, +30m, +1h, +2h) speed up common durations.
- **Date** — defaults to today; see [Backdating](#backdating) below.
- **Work type** — Remote, Onsite, After-hours, Travel, or Internal.
- **Billable flag** — whether the entry should be invoiced to the client.

A read-only strip on the modal shows your other entries for that day as positioned blocks, so you can sanity-check for overlaps before saving. All times are shown and interpreted in your organization's configured timezone, labeled at the top of the modal.

## Backdating

Time doesn't have to be logged the same day it happened. Change the **Date** field in the Log Time modal to log against any prior day — useful for catching up after a busy stretch or correcting a forgotten entry. Backdated entries still respect your organization's timezone when resolving which week they belong to.

## Billable vs non-billable

Mark entries as **billable** when the work should be invoiced to the client. Use **non-billable** for internal tasks, training, or work covered under a flat-rate agreement. Non-billable time still appears in reports for utilization tracking.

## Rate resolution

When a billable entry is invoiced, its hourly rate is resolved in this order — the first one that applies wins:

1. **Manual override** — an admin can set a specific rate on an entry when reviewing it, overriding everything else below.
2. **Service agreement rate** — the explicit or company-default [Service Agreement](/psa/projects-agreements) tied to the entry.
3. **Organization default rate** — a fallback hourly rate configured under Settings → Tickets → Billing.
4. **$0** — if none of the above apply.

## Approval workflow

Time entries move through a submit/approve cycle, with a rejection path that lets a technician correct and resubmit rather than dead-ending:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    The entry is created. The owner can edit or delete it while it's in this state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submitted">
    The technician submits the entry for review. It becomes read-only for the owner and appears in a manager's approval queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approved / Rejected">
    A manager or admin (someone other than the owner) either approves the entry, making it eligible for invoicing, or rejects it with a reason. A rejected entry can be edited and resubmitted — it isn't a dead end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoiced">
    Once attached to a generated invoice, the entry is locked into the billing record and shows a link to that invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Whether approval is required at all is configurable — see [Billing settings](#billing-settings) below. When it's turned off, submitted time is auto-approved (and audit-logged) at invoice time, which suits a solo shop with no separate approver.
</Note>

## Weekly Timesheets

**Timesheets** (PSA sidebar, under Finance) shows a Monday–Sunday grid of the tickets and projects you logged time against that week, with week navigation and — for managers — a technician selector to review someone else's week.

Below the grid, each entry has its own actions depending on status: **Edit** and **Submit** (draft or rejected entries), **Delete**, and, for managers, **Approve** or **Reject** with a reason.

The week itself has an envelope status of its own:

- **Submit week** — submits every draft or rejected entry in the week at once.
- **Approve week** — available to managers once every entry in the week is submitted; approves them all together. Blocked if any entry in the week is still a draft or rejected, so a week can't end up approved while part of it is still unresolved.
- **Reject week** — sends every submitted entry in the week back to rejected with a shared reason.

A rejected entry — whether rejected individually or as part of a week — can always be corrected and resubmitted. Once every entry in a previously-rejected week is resubmitted, the week's envelope automatically clears back to submitted so it's ready for approval again.

## Billing review ("Ready to Bill")

**Ready to Bill** (PSA sidebar, under Finance) is where approved, unbilled billable time turns into invoices. It groups outstanding time by company and [Service Agreement](/psa/projects-agreements) — a company's time with no agreement attached gets its own group — so you can see exactly what's ready to invoice before you generate anything.

Click **Create invoice** on any group to jump into the [invoice generation flow](/psa/invoices-quotes#how-to-generate-an-invoice), pre-filled with that company, agreement, and time period.

## Ticket billed notation and auto-close

When an invoice is generated from time entries, each affected ticket gets a system reply noting what was billed (e.g. "Time billed on invoice INV-2026-0042 ($240.00)."), and its unbilled-time total is updated.

If a ticket is **Resolved** and every bit of its billable time has now been billed, it automatically closes — provided **Auto-close tickets when billed** is turned on (it is by default). This closure doesn't notify the customer; it's treated as an internal, administrative step rather than a customer-facing status change.

## Billing settings

Under **Settings → Tickets → Billing**:

- **Auto-close tickets when billed** — default on. When an invoice covers all of a Resolved ticket's time, close it automatically.
- **Require approval before billing** — default on. Only approved time can be invoiced. Turn this off for a solo shop with no separate approver; submitted time is then auto-approved (and audit-logged) at invoice time.
- **Default hourly rate** — applied when a time entry has no service agreement rate to inherit. Leave blank for $0.

## Service agreements and payment

[Service Agreements](/psa/projects-agreements) define recurring and hourly billing structure per client, and time entries roll up into them automatically. Once entries are approved and invoiced, payment is collected through whichever [payment processor your organization has active](/psa/invoices-quotes#payment-processors) — Stripe, QuickBooks, or Alternative Payments.

<Warning>
  Invoices with $0 line items for time usually mean the included entries are either not approved or not marked as billable. Check entry status on Timesheets before generating.
</Warning>
