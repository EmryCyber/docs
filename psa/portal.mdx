Each client gets a dedicated support portal — a white-label interface where their end users can submit tickets, chat with your team, browse KB articles, and view their assets. The portal runs on your custom domain and carries your branding.

## Portal features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Submit tickets" icon="ticket">
    End users create support requests with title, description, urgency, an optional asset link (from their organization's inventory), and file attachments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="View ticket status" icon="list-check">
    Customers see real-time status updates on their open and resolved tickets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live chat" icon="comments">
    Real-time messaging with your technicians. Starting a chat creates a ticket immediately, with the opening message as its first reply.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Knowledge base" icon="book">
    Public KB articles are searchable and browsable from the portal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Asset view" icon="laptop">
    Customers see devices associated with their account, synced from Entra ID, Level.io, and NinjaOne.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Custom branding

Customize the portal appearance under **PSA → Support Portal** (also reachable from the **Support Portal** tile on the main Settings page):

- **Logo** — Displayed in the portal header and login page
- **Colors** — Primary and accent colors applied to buttons, links, and headers
- **Custom CSS** — Override any portal style with your own CSS for full control

<Tip>
  There is one support portal per MSP organization — every one of your clients signs in to the same branded portal (differentiated by their own login, not by separate per-client branding). Branding fields live on your organization, not per client.
</Tip>

## Request catalog and approvals

Enable the optional [Request catalog](/psa/request-catalog) to offer focused forms instead of only the general ticket form. Items can vary by company and portal role. Approver and Client admin contacts can use supported Requested for fields to raise work for colleagues; manager and role relationships determine approval audiences.

The **Approvals** portal page lets eligible contacts decide requests addressed to them. Requesters normally see In Progress while approval is pending, or Awaiting approval when the item opts in. Internal fulfilment task tickets remain staff-only.

## Home page and urgency wording

Under Support Portal settings, customize the home headline, supporting text, tiles, counters and recent tickets. Tiles can link to visible catalog categories/items or HTTPS destinations; customers do not gain access to another company's request item through a tile.

Customize the three urgency answers to language customers understand. This changes wording rather than the urgency/impact priority matrix; customers still cannot set Critical directly. Labels that masquerade as a priority are refused.

Logo backgrounds can use system, white, black or transparent presentation. Preview narrow screens as well as desktop; the portal is shared across your MSP's client companies, not a separate branding configuration for each one.

## Custom domain setup

Host your portal on your own domain (e.g., `support.yourmspname.com`), used by all of your clients:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the domain">
    Open **PSA → Support Portal**, scroll to the **Custom Domain** section, and enter the desired subdomain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure DNS">
    Add a CNAME record pointing your subdomain to the Regentra portal endpoint provided in the setup wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and activate">
    Regentra verifies DNS propagation and provisions an SSL certificate automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  DNS propagation can take up to 48 hours. The portal will not be accessible on the custom domain until verification completes.
</Warning>

## Portal authentication

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email / Password">
    There's no open self-registration — a technician creates the portal contact (manually or via Entra sync) and sends an invite; the end user follows the emailed link to set their own password.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Entra SSO">
    Customers with Microsoft Entra ID can sign in using their existing corporate credentials. This is a single portal-wide toggle in **PSA → Support Portal**, not configured per client. See [Microsoft Entra ID Integration](/integrations/microsoft-entra) for setup.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Contact management

Customer access follows the contact's current company and role. A retained session does not give access to a company the contact has left. Internal notes, routing decisions and fulfilment task tickets remain staff-only.

Portal contacts are the end users who can access the portal. Contacts can be:

- **Manually created** on the PSA → [Contacts](/psa/companies-contacts) page
- **Synced from Microsoft Entra ID** — Users from the client's Entra tenant are automatically imported as portal contacts

<Info>
  Synced contacts update automatically every 4 hours. Changes to user attributes in Entra (name, email, department) are reflected in the portal on the next sync.
</Info>
