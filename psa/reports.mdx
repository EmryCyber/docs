Regentra provides service-desk reports, a daily ticket ledger and routing distribution. Preview the report and use its available export formats before sharing it.

## Report types

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Technician Scorecard">
    Performance metrics for individual technicians over a selected period:

    - Tickets resolved
    - Average response time
    - Average resolution time
    - SLA compliance rate
    - Billable hours logged
    - CSAT score (per technician)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Client Report">
    Service delivery summary for a specific client:

    - Total ticket count, with a breakdown by status, priority, category, and source
    - SLA compliance percentage, plus a list of breached tickets
    - Average resolution time
    - Top requesters (contacts with the most tickets)
    - Billable vs. total hours logged
    - CSAT score (per client)
    - Automation value — tickets auto-resolved, technician hours avoided, and an estimated dollar value
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Service Desk Overview">
    Aggregate view of the entire service desk:

    - Total ticket volume, daily average, and trend
    - Breakdown by status, priority, source (channel), and category
    - SLA compliance across all clients
    - Average time to resolution
    - Backlog (open/unsolved count) and ticket aging
    - Technician leaderboard (resolved, avg resolution, CSAT, SLA%) and a per-client breakdown
    - Tickets auto-resolved by automation, and the technician hours that saved
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Daily ticket report

Open **PSA Reports → Daily ticket report** and choose an organization-local day, normally yesterday. It shows open at the start, new, resolved, closed, reopened and open at the end, with technician/group/company/contact-method breakdowns and a ticket table.

Reports distinguish recorded history from older reconstructed data. A report before lifecycle capture is not a claim that every historical transition was observed precisely. The daily PDF includes a bounded ticket table; use Excel for the complete ticket list.

A daily schedule can send yesterday's report at a chosen organization-local hour to selected active staff. Recipient eligibility is checked again at send time. This is separate from the monthly client reports below.

## Ticket distribution

Open **PSA Reports → Ticket distribution** for group assignments, overflow, waiting work and why automatic routing passed over members. Review current open and priority-weighted load. **Release tickets** previews work that will be returned to queues before confirmation. See [Ticket routing](/psa/ticket-routing).

## Lifecycle metrics and exports

Response, first/full resolution, reopen, assignment/group-change, on-hold and SLA outcomes are available through ticket metrics/history surfaces and supported exports. Internal notes do not count as a customer first response. Recurring maintenance and fulfilment tasks do not inflate customer SLA performance.

Under **Settings → Import / Export**, select export columns, save an export definition and optionally schedule it. Ticket history and Ticket time by technician are separate datasets. History quality labels identify reconstructed or pre-capture records. See [Import / Export](/settings/import-export#saved-and-scheduled-exports).

## Date ranges

Select from preset ranges or define a custom period:

- **Last 7d**
- **Last 30d** (default)
- **This Month**
- **Last Month**
- **This Quarter**
- **Custom** — Pick specific start and end dates

All reports respect your organization's configured timezone.

## Generating a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select report type">
    Navigate to **PSA Reports** in the sidebar (distinct from the compliance **Reports** page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set parameters">
    Choose the date range and, if applicable, the technician or client to report on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    The report renders on screen. Review the data before exporting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download PDF">
    Click **Download PDF** to save the report. The PDF matches the on-screen preview exactly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Client Service Reports are useful for quarterly business reviews. Generate them ahead of client meetings to present objective service delivery data.
</Tip>

## Scheduled reports

Regentra can generate and email two kinds of monthly reports automatically, from the **Scheduled Reports** panel at the top of the Reports page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Client monthly report">
    A branded, client-facing report for one company — the previous full calendar month's service data, formatted the same way as the Client Report above. Pick the **Company**, then check off the specific **Recipient Contacts** who should receive it (not "everyone at the company" — you choose exactly who). Recipients are re-verified against that company's contact list every time the report sends, so removing or deactivating a contact stops delivery to them automatically. You can add an optional note from your MSP that's included in the email.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Overview monthly digest">
    An internal Service Desk Overview PDF sent to every admin who has opted in via their **Monthly Report** email preference (Settings → Notifications). No recipients to pick — it's resolved per admin at send time.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Each schedule shows its next and last send date, and can be paused or deleted at any time without losing its configuration.

Client reports are white-label branded with your organization's logo and accent color, and include hours billed against each client's included agreement hours where a [Service Agreement](/psa/projects-agreements) applies. A month with no reportable activity is skipped rather than sending an empty report.

<Note>
  Scheduled Reports requires a Manager-level role or above to view and manage — it isn't available to plain technicians.
</Note>
