title: "Automations & AI Reviews"
description: "Automate ticket triage and assignment; review AI-suggested replies before they reach the customer."

Reduce manual work with automation rules that react to ticket events. Review AI-generated replies before they're sent so you stay in control of the customer experience.

## What it does

**Automations:**
- **Trigger events** — react to ticket creation, status changes, replies, assignment, SLA escalation, invoice generation, CSAT received, or a quote being accepted/declined
- **Conditions** — match on priority, status, category, contact email, company, and more
- **Actions** — auto-assign, change priority, update status, add internal notes, send notifications, or send a webhook
- **Rule priority** — execute rules in order; stop processing after a match if enabled
- **Execution logs** — view every time a rule ran, whether it matched, and what it did

**AI Reviews (admin-only):**
- **Pending reviews** — see tickets where a technician has already acted on an AI draft, sorted by confidence (lowest first), awaiting a manager's confirm-or-override
- **Manager confirm/override** — mark the tech's decision Correct, or override it with Override: AI Was Right / Override: AI Was Wrong
- **Audit trail** — every review is written to the audit log, and Recent Reviews shows the tech's action next to the manager's

## How to create an automation rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Automations and click New Rule">
    In the PSA module sidebar, click **Automations**, then click **New Rule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe the rule">
    Enter a name (e.g. "Auto-assign Critical tickets to Tom") and optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a trigger event">
    Ticket Created, Ticket Status Changed, Ticket Reply Added, Ticket Assigned, SLA Escalation, Invoice Generated, CSAT Received, or Quote Decided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions (optional)">
    Click **+ Add Condition**. For each, choose a field (e.g. Priority), operator (Equals), and value (CRITICAL). All conditions must match for the rule to execute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add actions">
    Click **+ Add Action**. Choose: Assign Ticket, Change Priority, Change Status, Add Internal Note, Send Notification, or Send Webhook. Configure the payload (e.g. which technician).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set priority and options">
    Lower Priority = executed first. Check **Enabled** to activate. Check **Stop after match** to prevent other rules from running if this one matches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule">
    Click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to test an automation rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the rule">
    Find the rule in your Automations list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the test icon">
    A dry-run executes the rule against a sample ticket. You'll see the test ticket evaluated, which conditions matched, what actions would have executed, and a "Would Execute" / "Would NOT execute" summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and adjust">
    If the test shows unexpected behavior, edit the conditions or actions and test again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How a technician handles an AI draft

On a ticket with a suggested reply, the technician reviews the draft text directly on the ticket (editing it in place changes nothing about the review process below — it's just the reply text) and either clicks **Send to Customer** (then confirms with **Confirm and Send**, **Send & Resolve**, or **Send & Close**) or **Discard**. This decision — accepted as-is, edited, or discarded — is what later shows up as the "tech action" in AI Reviews.

## How admin review works (AI Reviews)

**AI Reviews** is a retrospective quality-control page for admins, not a pre-send approval gate — by the time a ticket shows up here, the technician has already sent or discarded the reply.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Reviews">
    In the PSA sidebar, click **AI Reviews** (admin-only entry, near the bottom of the sidebar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="View pending reviews">
    The **Pending Reviews** card lists tickets whose AI draft was acted on by a technician but not yet reviewed by a manager, sorted by confidence (lowest first).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a ticket">
    Click a ticket to go to its detail page, where a **Manager Review** card shows the tech's action (Accepted / Edited / Discarded / Auto-sent) and the AI's confidence score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm or override">
    - **Correct** — the tech's decision was the right call.
    - **Override: AI Was Right** — the tech rejected or discarded a draft that was actually good.
    - **Override: AI Was Wrong** — the tech accepted a draft that was actually bad.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Overriding a rating updates that ticket's stored quality score, which weights how likely it is to be pulled back in as an example for future AI drafts — it isn't a fine-tuning or retraining step on the underlying model.
</Note>

## Understanding AI confidence

AI replies include a confidence score (0-100%) representing how sure the model is. Low scores mean the AI was uncertain — review carefully. **Low confidence does not always mean wrong; high confidence does not guarantee correct.** Always review the actual reply text and the ticket context before sending.

## AI and ticket priority

The AI auto-analyzer suggests, **never sets**, ticket priority. The priority chosen at ticket creation — by the customer (portal or Teams bot), by the inbound-email classifier, or by the operator (manual UI) — is the sole source of truth.

| Surface | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| AI's per-ticket triage analysis | Stored in `aiAnalysis.suggestedPriority`, visible in the ticket sidebar with an Apply control |
| Automatic write to `ticket.priority` from the AI analyzer | **Never happens.** No upgrade, no downgrade — the AI cannot mutate priority on its own |
| Operator-authored automation rule with **Change Priority** action | Allowed. MSP rules are explicit operator policy and remain authoritative |
| VIP override | Bumps LOW/MEDIUM → HIGH for VIP-tagged contacts (never downgrades) |
| Ticket-spike pattern detection | Bumps LOW/MEDIUM → HIGH when 3+ tickets arrive from the same company within 2 hours (never downgrades) |
| AI escalation step (security or high-frustration concern) | Bumps LOW/MEDIUM → HIGH AND flips status to **Escalated** with a visible audit reply explaining why. Never downgrades a customer-set CRITICAL or HIGH |

<Note>
  This is a hard guarantee. A customer-submitted CRITICAL ticket will arrive in the PSA at CRITICAL regardless of what the AI's per-ticket analysis suggests. Notification rules and SLA timers keyed on customer priority always fire correctly.
</Note>

<Info>
  The **Change Priority** automation action listed in the rule-builder above is for operator-authored MSP rules. It does not represent AI behavior. Use it freely — your rules are policy you define, not AI judgment.
</Info>

## Automation Value

Automation Value (**PSA sidebar → Automation Value**) tracks how many technician-minutes each platform automation saves, so client reports can show the true value of auto-resolved work instead of just a ticket count.

Every automation signal Regentra ships has a platform default — a reasonable estimate of how long the equivalent manual work would take. You can override any of them for your organization:

- Browse **Platform Defaults** to see the default minutes for each automation signal.
- Click **Override** to set your own **manual minutes equivalent** for your organization, plus an optional **fixed hourly rate** (otherwise the client's own agreement rate is used).
- Overridden signals show an **Overridden** badge; remove an override at any time to fall back to the platform default.

If a signal has no override and no platform default, Regentra falls back to a global default shown at the top of the page. Per-rule automations you build yourself (Settings → Automations) set their own minutes directly on the rule rather than through this page.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I test a rule without creating it?">
    No. Save the rule first, then use the test button. Drafts cannot be tested.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if two rules match the same ticket?">
    Rules execute in priority order (lower number = first). If a rule has **Stop after match** enabled, execution stops there. Otherwise all matching rules execute in sequence.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I disable a rule without deleting it?">
    Yes. Click the play/pause icon next to the rule. It's disabled immediately and won't execute on new tickets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are AI reviews separate from automations?">
    Yes. AI Reviews is an after-the-fact quality check on drafts technicians already accepted, edited, or discarded — it does not gate whether a reply is sent. Automations execute immediately with no manual review either way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the AI learn from my overrides?">
    Not in the sense of retraining the model. Marking a review Correct or Override updates that ticket's stored quality score, which influences whether it's pulled in as an example for future AI drafts (via retrieval), rather than fine-tuning the underlying AI model.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
