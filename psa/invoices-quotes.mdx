Create and manage customer quotes and invoices. Pull line items from your [Service Catalog](/psa/service-catalog), set payment terms, and send via your organization's active payment processor for hosted online payment or QuickBooks for accounting sync.

## What it does

- **Quote drafting** — Build multi-line quotes with custom descriptions, quantities, and pricing
- **Invoice generation** — Convert service agreements, logged time, or manual line items into invoices
- **Multiple destinations** — Send invoices to your active payment processor (for online payment) and/or QuickBooks (for accounting)
- **Catalog integration** — Pick from your [Service Catalog](/psa/service-catalog) or enter free-text items
- **Payment options** — Card, ACH, bank transfer, SEPA debit, and more per invoice
- **Tax & due date control** — Set tax rate and override the default 30-day due window
- **White-label branding** — Your logo, address, and tax details appear on every quote and invoice you send

## How to create a quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quotes and click New Quote">
    In the PSA module sidebar, click **Quotes**, then click the **New Quote** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill quote basics">
    Enter a title (e.g. "Managed IT Services Proposal"), select a [company and contact](/psa/companies-contacts) (optional), and set a validity date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items">
    Click **Add Item**. For each row, enter description, quantity, and unit price. Total calculates automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set tax and review totals">
    Enter a tax rate (%). Subtotal, tax amount, and grand total update in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the quote">
    Click **Create Quote**. The detail page lets you review, send, or edit before acceptance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quote recipient (Bill to)

Every quote has its own **Recipient / Bill to** block, independent of the company and contact you pick. This is what actually appears on the public quote page, the quote PDF, and the quote email — so even a quote with no company attached still has somewhere to put a full billing address.

Fill in name, email, phone, and address directly, or click **Use company details** to pull them from the company and contact you've already selected. An override always takes precedence over the company/contact fields on every surface; leaving a field blank falls back to the corresponding company or contact value.

<Note>
  Once a quote is **accepted** or **converted** to an invoice, the recipient block (along with the linked company and contact) is frozen — it can no longer be edited. This keeps the billing record on an accepted quote immutable even if the underlying company or contact record changes later.
</Note>

## How to generate an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Invoices and click Generate Invoice">
    In the PSA module sidebar, click **Invoices**, then click **Generate Invoice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose destination(s)">
    Check **Collect payment via `<your active processor>`** (for hosted online payment) and/or **QuickBooks** (for accounting sync). If QuickBooks is checked, link a Portal Company; click **Sync now** if not yet synced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a client and agreement (optional)">
    For your payment processor, pick a customer from your connected account. Optionally link a [Service Agreement](/psa/projects-agreements) to auto-populate recurring charges, or pull in [approved unbilled time](/psa/time-tracking#billing-review-ready-to-bill).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set period and line items">
    Define the billing period. Add manual line items by picking from your [Service Catalog](/psa/service-catalog) or entering free-text descriptions and amounts. Catalog items sync to your processor automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure payment options">
    Set a custom due date (default: 30 days from invoice date). If Stripe is your active processor, enable payment methods: Card, ACH, Link, Bank Transfer, SEPA Debit. If none selected, Card and ACH are used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the invoice">
    Click **Generate**. The invoice is created and synced to your chosen destination(s).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment processors

Regentra separates payment integrations into two roles, and enforces **at most one active provider per role** for your organization:

- **Processor** — collects payment from the customer. Stripe or Alternative Payments.
- **Accounting** — records the invoice to your books. QuickBooks or Xero.

QuickBooks is the one provider that can hold both roles at once — connecting it claims the Accounting slot automatically, and also claims the Processor slot if nothing else already holds it.

Connect a processor or accounting integration under **Settings → Integrations**. If you try to connect a second provider for a role that's already occupied, Regentra shows a confirmation naming the current holder and what switching means for existing invoices, rather than silently overriding it — you have to confirm the switch explicitly.

Whichever processor is active drives the invoice generation form: the checkbox reads **"Collect payment via `<your processor>`"** instead of a hard-coded provider name, and Stripe-specific options (customer picker, payment method selection) only appear when Stripe itself holds the Processor role.

<Note>
  Alternative Payments can be connected from Settings → Integrations, but its payment-collection adapter isn't live yet — it can hold the Processor role slot, but invoices can't currently be sent through it. Use Stripe for online payment collection today.
</Note>

## Importing invoices from Stripe

If you've ever billed a customer directly from the Stripe Dashboard rather than through Regentra, click **Import from Stripe** on the Invoices page to pull those invoices in. Each run:

- Looks back 90 days and imports up to 1,000 invoices by default.
- Matches each Stripe invoice to an existing customer where possible; unmatched customers are listed after the run with a **pick a company to link** shortcut, so future invoices for that customer resolve automatically.
- Is safe to re-run — an invoice already imported is updated in place rather than duplicated.
- Shows a **Run again to continue** link if it hits the 1,000-invoice cap, picking up exactly where it left off.

Imported invoices carry a **Stripe** badge to distinguish them from invoices you generated in Regentra. Because Stripe owns their content, a Stripe-origin invoice can't be edited or regenerated the way a Regentra-generated one can. Voiding one still works, but asks you to confirm you understand it originated in Stripe and that voiding here also voids it there — the confirmation names the Stripe invoice and links to it.

## QuickBooks push visibility

When your invoices should flow to QuickBooks automatically as they're created (rather than only on demand), turn on **auto-push** in your QuickBooks mapping settings. From that point forward, new invoices for QuickBooks-synced companies push automatically; invoices imported from Stripe *before* you turned this on are left alone so historical data isn't retroactively pushed.

To see what still needs attention:

- The **Not in QuickBooks** filter on the Invoices list shows every invoice that hasn't reached QuickBooks yet, with a badge explaining why — not yet synced, the company isn't linked to a QuickBooks customer, or the last push attempt failed (with the error shown on hover).
- **Send to QuickBooks**, available to admin roles, lets you select up to 50 not-yet-synced invoices at once and push them in a single action, rather than one at a time.
- A connection-health banner appears on the Invoices list itself (not just the Integrations page) if your QuickBooks connection needs to be reconnected.

## White-label branding

Your organization's logo, legal name, address, tax ID, and remit-to details — configured once under **Settings → Company Profile** — appear automatically on:

- The public quote page and the quote PDF
- The invoice PDF
- The quote-send email

You can also set default footer/memo text for quotes and invoices, and choose whether your tax ID appears on quotes. If you'd rather not show Regentra's own branding alongside yours, the **Show "Powered by Regentra"** toggle in the same settings controls that footer line across every one of these surfaces.

## Supply dates & tax compliance

When adding manual line items, optionally enable **Supply date** to specify a single date or date range (e.g. "Service period: 2026-05-01 — 2026-05-31"). This is rendered on the invoice and helps satisfy UK/EU tax-point rules.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I convert a quote to an invoice?">
    Yes. On an accepted quote's detail page, click **Convert to Invoice**. The new invoice copies the quote's line items and its resolved recipient ("Bill to") details, so the invoice matches exactly what the customer signed off on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I edit a catalog item?">
    The invoice retains the price and description at the time of generation. Historical invoices are unaffected. A new price is created on the next sync if the unit amount or currency changed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send an invoice to both my payment processor and QuickBooks?">
    Yes. Check both destinations, link a Portal Company for QuickBooks, and generate. The invoice is created in both systems independently.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Line Items and Service Agreements?">
    **Line Items** are one-off charges on a single invoice. **Service Agreements** ([Projects & Agreements](/psa/projects-agreements)) define recurring billing built from their own line items. You can link an agreement and still add extra line items.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
