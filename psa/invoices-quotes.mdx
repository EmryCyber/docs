Create and manage customer quotes and invoices. Pull line items from your [Service Catalog](/psa/service-catalog), set payment terms, and send via your organization's active payment processor for hosted online payment or QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books or Xero for accounting sync.

## What it does

- **Quote drafting** — Build multi-line quotes with custom descriptions, quantities, and pricing
- **Invoice generation** — Convert service agreements, logged time, or manual line items into invoices
- **Multiple destinations** — Send invoices to your active payment processor (for online payment) and/or your connected accounting system
- **Catalog integration** — Pick from your [Service Catalog](/psa/service-catalog) or enter free-text items
- **Payment options** — Card, ACH, bank transfer, SEPA debit, and more per invoice
- **Tax & due date control** — Set tax rate and override the default 30-day due window
- **White-label branding** — Your logo, address, and tax details appear on every quote and invoice you send

## How to create a quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quotes and click New Quote">
    In the PSA module sidebar, click **Quotes**, then click the **New Quote** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill quote basics">
    Enter a title (e.g. "Managed IT Services Proposal"), select a [company and contact](/psa/companies-contacts) (optional), and set a validity date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items">
    Click **Add Item**. For each row, enter description, quantity, and unit price. Total calculates automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set tax and review totals">
    Enter a tax rate (%). Subtotal, tax amount, and grand total update in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the quote">
    Click **Create Quote**. The detail page lets you review, send, or edit before acceptance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quote recipient (Bill to)

Every quote has its own **Recipient / Bill to** block, independent of the company and contact you pick. This is what actually appears on the public quote page, the quote PDF, and the quote email — so even a quote with no company attached still has somewhere to put a full billing address.

Fill in name, email, phone, and address directly, or click **Use company details** to pull them from the company and contact you've already selected. An override always takes precedence over the company/contact fields on every surface; leaving a field blank falls back to the corresponding company or contact value.

<Note>
  Once a quote is **accepted** or **converted** to an invoice, the recipient block (along with the linked company and contact) is frozen — it can no longer be edited. This keeps the billing record on an accepted quote immutable even if the underlying company or contact record changes later.
</Note>

## How to generate an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Invoices and click Generate Invoice">
    In the PSA module sidebar, click **Invoices**, then click **Generate Invoice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose destination(s)">
    Choose from the connected destinations shown. A selected accounting destination needs a linked company/customer. Use the company card to initiate its first customer sync explicitly; creating a company does not create an accounting customer in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a client and agreement (optional)">
    Select the company and the appropriate linked provider customer. Optionally link a [Service Agreement](/psa/projects-agreements) to auto-populate recurring charges, or pull in [approved unbilled time](/psa/time-tracking#billing-review-ready-to-bill).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set period and line items">
    Define the billing period. Add manual line items by picking from your [Service Catalog](/psa/service-catalog) or entering free-text descriptions and amounts. Product descriptions, quantities, rates and periods are copied into editable invoice lines. Review customer-specific rates rather than assuming the catalog default is the agreed price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure payment options">
    Use the organization or client payment terms, or set a specific due date. If Stripe is your active processor, enable payment methods: Card, ACH, Link, Bank Transfer, SEPA Debit. If none selected, Card and ACH are used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the invoice">
    Use **Preview PDF** to inspect the current draft, then click **Generate**. If review is enabled, this creates a local invoice awaiting approval; otherwise the selected destination workflow runs. Read each destination result instead of assuming a failed provider send succeeded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saved drafts and PDF preview

The generation form supports saved drafts so you can return to unfinished work. Resolve a failed autosave or use its retry before leaving the page. Draft editing, duplicate-line controls and product selection do not change historical invoices.

Use **Preview PDF** on the live draft to review current lines, periods, recipient, terms and totals without issuing a number or marking time billed. Products can be created inline, and their long descriptions can be edited in an expanded, movable editor. Product headings print above the description; saved invoice lines preserve their heading snapshot.

## Review and release

Enable a company's **Financial settings** and select **Review and release before sending or syncing** to opt new invoices into review. Existing companies keep their current flow until opted in.

1. Generate a local Draft invoice. No customer email or provider invoice is created by that generation step.
2. Open it, edit permitted draft details and preview its PDF. Review invoices keep a revision so an approval cannot silently use stale edits.
3. In **Invoice review and release**, select up to 25 invoices and confirm the batch's customer-email choice.
4. **Approve and release** freezes those versions and sends them through the selected provider checks.
5. Use **Resume batch** after a partial failure. Review the invoice's release error; completed work is retained.

Approved invoices cannot be edited. If a replacement is needed, review voiding and any one-time agreement charges before preparing it. Recurring agreement quantities/prices are not repriced by editing the review invoice: change the agreement and regenerate where appropriate.

Regentra's native release email attaches its PDF and a payment link when available after provider validation. AP handles its own delivery. Provider-hosted invoices keep the provider's layout. A failed or uncertain send must be investigated before sending another copy manually.

<Warning>
  Review release can refuse accounting-managed tax totals or unsupported credit handling rather than email an unverified payable amount. Confirm tax in the issuing system. Choose Confirmed non-taxable services only when that is true; do not change tax treatment merely to bypass a release error. No new autopay or automatic-charge scheduler is enabled by this workflow.
</Warning>

## Payment terms and service periods

Client financial settings can override payment terms, a third-Monday due rule, PO/reference, message/footer, PDF layout and review defaults. A specific due date overrides its calculated rule. Service periods must end on or after their start date and print with the line details.

**Standard** and **Service details** PDFs share stored billing preferences. Service details provides boxed recipient/account fields and a detailed balance summary. A due date schedules when payment is owed; it does not authorize a charge. See [Invoice settings](/settings/invoices).

## Payment processors

Regentra separates payment integrations into two roles, and enforces **at most one active provider per role** for your organization:

- **Processor** — collects payment from the customer. Stripe, Alternative Payments, QuickBooks or Zoho Books.
- **Accounting** — records the invoice to your books. QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books or Xero.

QuickBooks and Zoho Books can hold both roles at once — connecting one claims the Accounting slot and claims the Processor slot only if it is free. Xero holds the Accounting role.

Connect a processor or accounting integration under **Settings → Integrations**. If you try to connect a second provider for a role that's already occupied, Regentra shows a confirmation naming the current holder and what switching means for existing invoices, rather than silently overriding it — you have to confirm the switch explicitly.

Whichever processor is active drives the invoice generation form: the checkbox reads **"Collect payment via `<your processor>`"** instead of a hard-coded provider name, and Stripe-specific options (customer picker, payment method selection) only appear when Stripe itself holds the Processor role.

<Note>
  [Alternative Payments](/integrations/alternative-payments) can collect through its accounting-system connection or receive invoices directly from Regentra when direct sending is enabled. Use one route for the same invoices. Direct sending supports USD and sends the AP copy on Send or an approved review release.
</Note>

## Importing invoices from Stripe

If you've ever billed a customer directly from the Stripe Dashboard rather than through Regentra, click **Import from Stripe** on the Invoices page to pull those invoices in. Each run:

- Looks back 90 days and imports up to 1,000 invoices by default.
- Matches each Stripe invoice to an existing customer where possible; unmatched customers are listed after the run with a **pick a company to link** shortcut, so future invoices for that customer resolve automatically.
- Is safe to re-run — an invoice already imported is updated in place rather than duplicated.
- Shows a **Run again to continue** link if it hits the 1,000-invoice cap, picking up exactly where it left off.

Imported invoices carry a **Stripe** badge to distinguish them from invoices you generated in Regentra. Because Stripe owns their content, a Stripe-origin invoice can't be edited or regenerated the way a Regentra-generated one can. Voiding one still works, but asks you to confirm you understand it originated in Stripe and that voiding here also voids it there — the confirmation names the Stripe invoice and links to it.

## QuickBooks push visibility

When your invoices should flow to QuickBooks automatically as they're created (rather than only on demand), turn on **auto-push** in your QuickBooks mapping settings. From that point forward, new invoices for QuickBooks-synced companies push automatically; invoices imported from Stripe *before* you turned this on are left alone so historical data isn't retroactively pushed.

To see what still needs attention:

- The **Not in QuickBooks** filter on the Invoices list shows every invoice that hasn't reached QuickBooks yet, with a badge explaining why — not yet synced, the company isn't linked to a QuickBooks customer, or the last push attempt failed (with the error shown on hover).
- **Send to QuickBooks**, available to admin roles, lets you select up to 50 not-yet-synced invoices at once and push them in a single action, rather than one at a time.
- A connection-health banner appears on the Invoices list itself (not just the Integrations page) if your QuickBooks connection needs to be reconnected.

## Accounting imports and review

QuickBooks invoice import is opt-in. A new Zoho organization connection starts a bounded customer/open-invoice import; older connections can start it with **Import customers and open invoices**. Imported invoices retain their source ownership; link an unmatched invoice to the correct company before attempting a new destination. Do not push a provider's imported invoice back to that same provider as a new copy.

The list shows provider status and combined **Not in** filters for connected accounting systems. Bulk sends report per-invoice errors and skip ineligible orphaned imports. Existing provider settings control whether new manual or monthly agreement invoices are sent automatically; turning on a new option does not send historical invoices indiscriminately.

Accounting-side voids and deletions enter a review queue. Administrators can apply or dismiss them after inspecting local invoices and payments. Review independent accounting receipts before deciding whether a status or payment should change.

## Payments recorded elsewhere

Recording a full or partial payment in the books can update Regentra and close overlapping Stripe/AP collection when appropriate. Split receipts from an accounting system and a processor can settle the same linked invoice; those reads keep provider identities separate.

Bridges are best effort. A local Paid status is not proof every accounting entry or remote collection-close succeeded. Inspect warnings and the provider's current state before manually collecting again. Refunds, disputes and later failed receipts do not automatically reopen invoices.

## White-label branding

Your organization's logo, legal name, address, tax ID, and remit-to details — configured once under **Settings → Company Profile** — appear automatically on:

- The public quote page and the quote PDF
- The invoice PDF
- The quote-send email

You can also set default footer/memo text for quotes and invoices, and choose whether your tax ID appears on quotes. If you'd rather not show Regentra's own branding alongside yours, the **Show "Powered by Regentra"** toggle in the same settings controls that footer line across every one of these surfaces.

## Supply dates & tax compliance

When adding manual line items, optionally enable **Supply date** to specify a single date or date range (e.g. "Service period: 2026-05-01 — 2026-05-31"). The saved service date or period is rendered on the invoice.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I convert a quote to an invoice?">
    Yes. On an accepted quote's detail page, click **Convert to Invoice**. The new invoice copies the quote's line items and its resolved recipient ("Bill to") details, so the invoice matches exactly what the customer signed off on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I edit a catalog item?">
    The invoice retains the price and description at the time of generation. Historical invoices are unaffected. A new price is created on the next sync if the unit amount or currency changed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send an invoice to both my payment processor and QuickBooks?">
    Yes, when both routes are supported and connected. Link the correct company/customer and review the selected destinations. With review enabled, delivery waits for approval and release. AP must not also import the same invoice through its accounting connection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Line Items and Service Agreements?">
    **Line Items** are one-off charges on a single invoice. **Service Agreements** ([Projects & Agreements](/psa/projects-agreements)) define recurring billing built from their own line items. You can link an agreement and still add extra line items.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
