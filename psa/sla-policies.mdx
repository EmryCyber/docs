title: "SLA Policies"
description: "Configure response and resolution targets, business calendars, escalations and internal group targets."
---

SLA policies define your customer service commitments. They match tickets by priority and optional company, type, category or urgency conditions, then track response and resolution separately.

## What SLAs track

- **Response** measures the time to the first qualifying staff reply. An internal note or simply moving a ticket to In Progress is not a customer response.
- **Resolution** measures covered time until resolution. Reopening a ticket resumes its resolution work; it does not erase a recorded breach or invent another first response.

Coverage can be business hours, extended business plus on-call hours, or 24/7, independently for each target. Recurring maintenance tickets and internal fulfilment tasks do not receive a customer SLA.

## Creating an SLA policy

1. Open **SLA Policies** and create a policy for a priority.
2. Set its company scope and optional matching conditions. Include child companies when appropriate.
3. Set response and resolution targets and their coverage.
4. Choose a named business calendar, inherited hours, or the policy's existing hours.
5. Set the at-risk threshold and any escalation levels, then save.

Policies are evaluated in configured order; the first active matching policy wins. Review ordering when several policies could match the same ticket. Customer-specific and general policies retain their ordering bands.

Existing policies keep their legacy hours unless you change their configuration. A policy already used by tickets is retired rather than removing its historical meaning.

## Business calendars and holidays

A business calendar holds a time zone and working windows for each weekday, including optional on-call windows. Holiday sets can close business hours, close all coverage, or substitute another weekday's hours for a full or partial day. Presets are available for supported national holiday sets and years.

Assign a calendar on a company's page or set an organization default. An inheriting policy uses the company's calendar, then a parent's, then the organization default; if none applies, it uses the policy's own hours. A named-calendar policy uses the selected calendar directly.

Calendar and holiday edits re-plan running clocks in the background, preserving time already used and recorded breaches. Calendar coverage is separate from appointment scheduling and technician availability.

## SLA statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **On Track** | The running target has time remaining. |
| **At Risk** | The configured warning threshold has been reached. |
| **Breached** | The deadline was exceeded. |
| **Met** | The completed target was satisfied in time. |

Response and resolution can have different outcomes. The ticket's **SLA details** card explains the selected policy, hours, targets, pauses, escalation activity and group-target segments. The list can filter At risk, Response breached, and Resolution breached separately.

A later resolution does not erase a recorded breach. Reports score response and resolution against their own targets; review the metric rather than treating every reopened ticket as a new SLA.

## SLA pausing

The built-in **Waiting on Customer** and **Waiting Approval** states pause the resolution clock. Custom statuses can have an allowed pause rule; changing between two custom statuses in the same category can therefore pause or resume it.

Open, Escalated, Resolved and Closed categories do not gain arbitrary pause behavior. Waiting Approval is controlled by approval workflows and always pauses. Response is measured to the first qualifying reply rather than paused whenever a ticket waits.

For an ad-hoc approval, an expired undecided link does not by itself move the ticket out of Waiting Approval. A catalog approval follows its configured expiry fallback. See [Ticket approvals](/psa/tickets#ticket-approvals) and [Request catalog approvals](/psa/request-catalog#approval-policies).

## Escalation notifications

Configure escalation levels for each response or resolution target. A level can run before or after its deadline, using covered time or elapsed time. Actions include notifications, reassignment, movement to a group, escalation to the next tier, raising priority, and supported status changes.

The SLA clock checker runs every minute; an hourly check remains as a backstop. When multiple levels have already passed at evaluation, the highest eligible level fires and lower ones are superseded. A configured level runs once for its clock.

When a metric has custom escalation levels, its default SLA email is suppressed to avoid sending both paths. Otherwise the default at-risk/breach audience applies. Personal notification settings and explicitly selected recipients affect delivery; the ticket's SLA details show what fired.

## Recalculation and policy edits

Changing a ticket's priority, type, category, company or urgency can select a new matching policy. Running targets carry forward time already consumed. An unchanged match leaves the deadline alone, and recorded breaches are retained.

Policy edits do not silently rewrite every running ticket. Administrators can use **Re-apply** to preview and apply the policy to existing tickets, or **Recalculate** on an individual ticket. Review the preview before applying a change that could affect deadlines.

## Group targets (OLA)

Under **SLA Policies → Group targets (OLA)**, set internal pick-up and resolution targets for [technician groups](/psa/ticket-routing), with their own calendar and escalation levels.

Each period a group holds a ticket is a separate segment. Moving the ticket away or resolving it ends that segment; reopening or entering another group can start another. OLA targets describe internal hand-offs and do not replace the customer's SLA. OLA reporting and outbound OLA events are not provided by the current workflow.

## SLA compliance reporting

Use **PSA Reports** to review response and resolution outcomes across clients and technicians. Tickets without an SLA are excluded from SLA compliance scoring. [Daily ticket reports and exports](/psa/reports) also provide lifecycle and history context; older reconstructed history is labeled rather than presented as exact recorded activity.
