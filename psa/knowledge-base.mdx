The knowledge base is a shared article library that serves two purposes: it gives your customers self-service answers through the portal, and it provides context for Regentra's AI-powered ticket draft feature.

## Creating articles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Knowledge Base">
    In the PSA module sidebar, click **Knowledge Base**, then click **New Article**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the article">
    Use the rich text editor to write your article. The toolbar supports bold/italic, headings (H1-H3), bulleted and numbered lists, block quotes, a horizontal rule, code blocks, and inline images.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose visibility">
    Pick one of three audiences:
    - **Internal (staff only)** — visible only to your team. Hidden from the customer portal.
    - **Customer (specific company)** — visible to a single client's portal users. Used for client-specific runbooks or onboarding docs.
    - **Global (all staff + portal)** — visible to your team and to every customer in the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to a folder">
    Organize the article into a folder for easier navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, then publish">
    Click **Save** — the article is created as a **Draft** and isn't available yet. Open it again and click **Publish** to make it available based on its visibility setting; the same button toggles a published article back to Draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organizing with folders

Folders group related articles into logical categories. Common structures include:

- **Getting Started** — Onboarding and setup guides
- **Account Management** — Password resets, MFA, user provisioning
- **Network & Connectivity** — VPN, Wi-Fi, DNS troubleshooting
- **Applications** — Per-application how-tos and known issues

Folders appear in the portal sidebar, letting customers browse by topic.

## Document attachments

Beyond markdown articles, the Knowledge Base can also hold uploaded files — PDFs, Office documents, Visio diagrams, and images, up to 50 MB each. Click **Upload** in the Documents panel, give the file a title (defaults to the file name), optionally scope it to a specific client, and it appears alongside the folder's articles for browsing or download.

Use document attachments for internal reference material that doesn't make sense as a markdown article — vendor-supplied PDFs, network diagrams, or signed client documents you want your team to find alongside your written articles. Document uploads are a staff-facing tool; they don't appear in the customer portal the way visibility-tagged markdown articles do.

## Turning a resolved ticket into a KB article

On a **Resolved** or **Closed** ticket's detail page, a "Create a KB article?" card offers to have AI draft a knowledge base article from the ticket. Click **Generate Draft** to review a title, excerpt, tags, and content preview.

<Info>
  If a similar existing article is found, the card flags it (with a similarity percentage) and offers **Update Existing Article** instead of creating a duplicate. Otherwise, click **Create New Article** to save the draft as a new article. New articles created this way are always **Internal (staff only)** — you can change visibility afterward by editing the article.
</Info>

There's no persistent link stored between the ticket and the article it was generated from — the connection is a one-time AI draft, not a lasting reference field you can browse from either side later.

## Portal-facing KB

Customers access the knowledge base through the [support portal](/psa/portal). They can:

- Browse articles by folder
- Search across all public articles
- View articles without opening a ticket

A well-maintained KB reduces ticket volume by enabling self-service resolution.

## AI-powered ticket drafts

Regentra's AI draft feature uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to suggest replies:

1. When a technician clicks **Analyze with AI** on a ticket, the AI searches your KB for relevant articles
2. It combines the KB content with the ticket context (subject, description, conversation history)
3. A draft reply is generated and shown to the technician for review
4. The technician can edit, send as-is, or discard the draft

<Tip>
  The quality of AI drafts improves directly with the breadth and accuracy of your knowledge base. Prioritize documenting your most common resolution steps.
</Tip>
