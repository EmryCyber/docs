---
title: "Quickstart — Your First 10 Minutes"
description: "Go from zero to managing compliance and service tickets in under 10 minutes."
---

This guide walks you through the essential setup steps so you can start using Regentra immediately. By the end, you will have an active compliance framework, a support ticket, and your team invited.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your organization">
    Go to [app.regentra.io](https://app.regentra.io) and click **Get Started**.

    You will need to provide:
    - **Organization name**
    - **Industry** — choose the closest match from a list covering Healthcare/Life Sciences, Financial Services/Fintech, Technology/SaaS, Government/Defense Contractor, Education, Retail/Ecommerce, Manufacturing/Industrial, Professional Services, and Other
    - **Admin details** — first name, last name, email, and a password (12 characters minimum; Regentra also screens passwords against known data breaches and rejects a match)
    - Agreement to the **Terms of Service and Privacy Policy** — a required checkbox

    <Note>
      Your organization is created as an isolated tenant. All data, controls, and configurations are scoped to your org. Every new organization starts with a 14-day trial.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the onboarding wizard">
    After registration, the onboarding wizard guides you through initial setup.

    First, confirm your **timezone** — this affects SLA business hours, report date ranges, and dashboard timestamps. Your browser's detected timezone is suggested as the default.

    Then it asks **how you'll use Regentra**:
    - **My own organization** — routes you straight into the Compliance module. There's no separate module picker at this step; a solo/individual org is compliance-focused by design.
    - **I manage clients** — the MSP path. You'll then choose what you're looking to test: **PSA / Service Desk** or **Both PSA + Compliance**. (An MSP that wants Compliance without PSA can pick Both and simply not use the PSA module, or add PSA later.) MSPs are also asked to create their first client during this flow, with the option to skip and do it later.

    Other org-level preferences (business hours, branding, custom CSS) are configured later from **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Adopt your first compliance framework">
    Navigate to the **Frameworks** page from the sidebar.

    1. Browse the available frameworks (HIPAA 2026, NIST CSF 2.0, SOC 2, ISO 27001, CMMC Level 1, CMMC Level 2, PCI DSS, GDPR, FTC Safeguards, SEC Regulation S-P, and FINRA)
    2. Click **Adopt** on the framework your client needs
    3. Regentra seeds the full control set for that framework into your organization
    4. The compliance dashboard populates with your gap analysis and control status breakdown

    <Tip>
      Start with the framework most relevant to your largest client. You can adopt additional frameworks at any time — the Common Control Framework maps overlapping controls automatically.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first support ticket">
    Switch to the **PSA** module and click **Tickets** in the sidebar, then **New Ticket**.

    Fill in the form:
    - **Company** *(required)* — pick an existing company or create a new one inline from the same dropdown
    - **Contact Name** *(required)* — the end user requesting support
    - **Contact Email** *(required)* — used for status notifications
    - **Title** *(required)* — short summary of the issue
    - **Description** *(required)* — details about the request or incident
    - **Priority** — Low, Medium, High, or Critical (defaults to Medium)
    - **Category** — optional free-text label (e.g., "Billing", "Network", "Access")
    - **Assign To** — assign to yourself or leave Unassigned for the queue

    Submit the ticket to see it appear in your queue with SLA timers active.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Go to **Settings → Team Members** and click **Invite Member**.

    Enter an email address and assign one of nine assignable roles. The most common starting roles are:
    - **Admin** — full access to all modules and settings
    - **Approver** — reviews and approves policies, controls, and assessments (the COMPLIANCE_OFFICER role)
    - **Analyst** — does compliance work but cannot approve (the EMPLOYEE role)
    - **MSP Technician** — handles tickets and service-desk operations
    - **Read Only** — view-only access for auditors or executives

    See [Concepts → Roles](/concepts#roles) for the full list, including the privacy- and security-focused approver roles and the MSP-tier admin roles.

    <Info>
      Invited users receive an email with a link to set their password and join your organization.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect an integration">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and connect your first external service.

    **Microsoft Entra ID** — sync your user directory, pull MFA status, and collect identity-related compliance evidence automatically.

    **Level.io** — import device inventory and asset data into the PSA module from your RMM.

    Follow the guided setup for each integration. Most connections take under two minutes to configure.

    <Warning>
      Integration credentials are encrypted at rest and scoped to your organization. Only admins can configure integrations.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>
