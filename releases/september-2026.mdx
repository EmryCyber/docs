September was our biggest month yet. This page is a round-up of everything that shipped, grouped by area. For the week-by-week detail, including anything you need to do, see the [Changelog](/changelog).

<Note>
  **Things to check after this release**

  - **Sender display name:** rename your support mailbox in the Exchange admin center to change the name customers see.
  - **Work notes on invoices** are now off by default. Turn them on in **Settings → Tickets → Billing** if you want them.
  - **Regentra bot in Teams** works in one-to-one chats only. Use the Teams Chat Bridge for group chats.
</Note>

## Ticketing

- **Ticket types**: classify tickets. Templates, checklists and custom fields can each be tied to a type.
- **Ticket templates**: start a new ticket from a saved template.
- **Checklists**: optionally warn or block before a ticket with open required items is resolved.
- **Custom fields**: grouped into sections, with optional fields on the customer portal.
- **Recurring tickets**: create tickets automatically on a daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly schedule.
- **Urgency × impact priority**: customers now say how urgent their issue is, and technicians set the final priority. This also applies to the Teams tab.
- **Resolve/Close dialog**: separate boxes for an internal resolution note and a message to the customer.
- **Me / Unassigned / Team views** for the ticket list, with a per-user default.
- **Saved ticket list filters**: organization-wide and personal defaults. Backing out of a ticket returns you to your filtered list.
- **Link tickets to projects**: project cards now show the time logged on their tickets.
- **@mentions**: work in new ticket descriptions and in replies. Saved responses now show up in the reply editor.
- **AI "Find a ticket" search**: describe a past ticket in plain words and find it.
- **Automatic device linking**: picking a contact on a ticket links their device.
- **CSAT drill-down**: the dashboard's satisfaction cards are clickable, and each ticket shows its survey result.
- **Priority protection**: a priority a technician sets by hand is no longer changed by automation or AI. Priority is now edited from the ticket header.
- **Getting Started checklist** for new organizations. Its progress comes from what's actually set up, not from boxes ticked.

## Time tracking and timesheets

- **Timer and weekly timesheets**, with approval of a whole week at once.
- **Ready to Bill** screen.
- **Tickets close automatically once their time is billed** (can be turned off).
- **Backdated time**: log time on past days.
- **Time-entry presets** and a **rate card** by type of work.
- **New Timesheets page**: weeks are shown as a list, and managers can submit, approve or reject on a technician's behalf.
- **New time-entry panel on tickets**: an interactive timeline, editing of entries, and a better phone layout.
- **Corrections**: submitted time can be taken back to draft and corrected. Week status stays in step with individual entries.

[Time tracking guide](/psa/time-tracking)

## Billing, invoices and quotes

- **Products on time entries**: each time entry becomes its own invoice line.
- **Bill straight from Timesheets.**
- **Edit invoice lines before generating**: change hours, rates and descriptions. The form explains time that came in at $0.
- **More detailed line items**: quantity, delivery periods, a searchable product picker and favorite products.
- **Product CSV import and export**, with optional SKUs and better Sync from Stripe.
- **Stripe invoices**: invoices created directly in Stripe now import into the PSA.
- **QuickBooks**: each invoice shows whether it reached QuickBooks, and invoices can be sent there in bulk.
- **Payment processors**: Stripe and QuickBooks each have a clear role. Payments, refunds and chargebacks are recorded.
- **Redesigned quotes**: branded quote and invoice PDFs, plus "Bill to" details on quotes.
- **Service agreement fixes**:
  - Recurring charges bill once per month.
  - Only the unbilled part of a month's charge is billed.
  - Invoice lines always add up to the total.
- **QuickBooks sales tax** is kept when QuickBooks collects the payment.
- **Technicians' work notes** are left off customer invoices by default.

## Integrations

- **Hudu**:
  - Integration enabled.
  - Networks, VLANs and IP addresses sync every 15 minutes.
  - Your own edits are protected from being overwritten.
- **Level and NinjaOne**: renames and deletions are detected, with a review queue for them.
- **Level alerts**: open and resolved alerts are re-checked every hour, so a missed alert notification no longer leaves a ticket missing or stuck open.
- **Stripe and QuickBooks**: customer name changes flow back into the PSA.
- **Microsoft Entra**:
  - Deleted users are deactivated, and can be restored.
  - Stale devices are handled.
  - New device import settings.
- **Asset details**: last seen, logged-in user, security score, OS and compliance state are now real fields.
- **Device owners**: a device's owner now follows whoever actually uses it.
- **Company page**: shows RMM cards only for the RMM the company uses, adds a Level card, and shows "Last synced" times.
- **Teams app** overhaul.
- **Accurate setup guides** for every integration.

## Account, settings and onboarding

- **New "My account" area**: your profile, preferences, notifications, security and calendar in one place.
- **Settings** is now its own area, separate from the PSA and Compliance apps.
- **Email verification** comes before passkey setup, and the verification link works on any device.
- **Onboarding** is now a guide rather than a requirement. Nothing is blocked while it's unfinished.
- **Trials**:
  - Live countdown banner.
  - Option to extend by 7 days.
  - Manual extensions by support.
  - A free week offered when you cancel.
  - Clearer messages when a trial has expired.
- **Billing fixes**:
  - Compliance-only organizations can buy PSA.
  - Buying one product no longer causes problems with your other trial.
  - Cancelled customers are no longer treated as expired trials.

## Security and privacy

- **Four security audits** completed and their findings fixed.
- **Microsoft tenant connections** now require proof of admin consent.
- **Internal notes stay private**: they are kept out of chat transcripts and AI reply drafts, and screenshots pasted into them stay off the customer portal.
- **AI reply drafts** only use the same customer's past tickets.
- **Device syncs** can no longer affect another client's assets.
- **Logged time is protected**: deleting a user or project can no longer erase it.
- **Activity feed**: visibility is based on role.

## Reliability fixes

- **Inbound email**:
  - No more lost mail.
  - A retry queue for failed messages.
  - Attachment downloads have more time to finish.
- **SLA timers** stay correct across daylight saving time changes.
- **Portal links in ticket emails** work when signed out and on custom domains.
- **Contacts**:
  - Searching works across the whole list.
  - Email matching ignores upper/lower case.
  - Deactivating a contact saves immediately.
- **Scheduled reports** work for PSA-only organizations.
- **Layout**: fixes for phones and smaller screens across the app.
- **Crash fixes**: Super Admin pages, and invisible buttons on sign-in pages.
