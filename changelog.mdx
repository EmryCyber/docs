## 2026-09

### Payment processors: choose your own processor and accounting sync

Payment integrations are no longer hard-coded to Stripe. Regentra now models them by role: a **processor** that actually collects payment (Stripe, or the new Alternative Payments option) and an **accounting sync** that records it (QuickBooks or Xero). Only one processor and one accounting sync can be active at a time, and QuickBooks can serve as both. Connecting a new processor while one is already active shows a confirmation dialog before switching, so you never silently lose a connection mid-billing-cycle.

[Payment processors guide](/integrations/payment-processors)

### Incident Management: guided intake-to-report workflow

Incident reporting is now a full lifecycle, not a bare form. A new intake flow captures what happened, when, and how it was detected; Regentra proposes notification obligations and deadlines based on the incident's data types and your adopted frameworks (HIPAA, FTC Safeguards, SEC Reg S-P, GDPR, CMMC, and PCI DSS); a four-factor HIPAA breach-risk assessment computes a Breach / Not a Breach / Pending determination; and a one-click regulator-ready PDF report — with an append-only timeline, chain-of-custody evidence log, and corrective actions — can be downloaded or emailed directly to your legal or compliance contacts.

[Incident Management guide](/compliance/incidents)

### PSA time tracking → invoicing

Time tracking is now a full timer-to-invoice workflow. **Timesheets** (PSA → Finance) is a weekly grid with submit/approve/reject at the week level — rejected entries can be corrected and resubmitted instead of dead-ending. A new **Ready to Bill** screen groups approved, unbilled time by company and agreement for guided invoice creation. Billing a ticket's time now posts an automatic note on the ticket and, once nothing is left unbilled, can auto-close a resolved ticket (configurable in Settings → Tickets).

[Time tracking guide](/psa/time-tracking)

### Networks: subnets, VLANs, and IP tracking for your clients

A new Networks section (PSA → Knowledge) tracks subnets, VLANs, and IP addresses per client, with CSV import/export and a one-way migration path from Hudu for MSPs moving off it.

[Networks guide](/psa/networks) · [Hudu import guide](/integrations/hudu)

### Data Export: CSV, XLSX, and PDF for every dataset

Every PSA and compliance dataset — companies, tickets, invoices, quotes, agreements, projects, controls, policies, vendors, risks, the audit log, and more — can now be exported in CSV, XLSX, or PDF from one place. Large exports automatically switch to a background job that emails you a download link instead of timing out.

[Data export guide](/settings/import-export)

### Importing Stripe-Dashboard invoices; QuickBooks push visibility

Invoices billed directly from the Stripe Dashboard — outside PSA — now import into Regentra automatically, so your invoice list reflects everything you've actually billed. An **Import from Stripe** button backfills recent history on demand, and unmatched Stripe customers surface a direct link to connect them to a company. On the QuickBooks side, a **"Not in QuickBooks"** filter, a per-invoice error message, and a bulk **Send to QuickBooks** action (for admins) replace the old one-shot toast for push failures.

[Stripe integration guide](/integrations/stripe) · [QuickBooks integration guide](/integrations/quickbooks)

### White-label branding on quotes, invoices, and client-facing documents

Company Profile is now one consolidated settings page that drives your logo, colors, and business details across every customer-facing document: quote pages, quote PDFs, invoice PDFs, quote emails, client reports, and public shared-link pages. Quotes also gained explicit "Bill to" overrides — set a name, email, phone, or address on the quote itself, independent of the linked company, and it's preserved even if that company record changes later. An accepted or converted quote's billing details are now frozen against further edits.

[Organization Profile guide](/settings/organization) · [Quotes & invoices guide](/psa/invoices-quotes)

### Smaller fixes

- The ticket list's "All" filter card now reliably clears every other filter and search term in one click.
- Compliance monitoring findings on organizations running both Compliance and PSA now file into PSA tickets automatically, grouped into one rollup ticket instead of flooding the queue.
- Compliance-only organizations can now subscribe to PSA directly from Billing.
- Various responsive/overflow fixes across ticket, invoice, and toolbar layouts on narrower screens.

## 2026-08

### NIST CSF 2.0 Tiers self-assessment; framework-first presentation

Organizations that adopt NIST CSF 2.0 can now complete an official Tiers self-assessment (Partial through Adaptive) alongside their control work. Across the Compliance module, control detail pages, the framework view, and the NIST CSF category headers now lead with the **selected framework's own** identifiers and requirement text — Regentra's internal control taxonomy is still visible but clearly labeled as Regentra's own mapping layer, not the framework's structure.

[Frameworks guide](/compliance/frameworks)

### FINRA added as a supported framework

**FINRA Member Firm Compliance** joins the framework library, bringing the total to eleven supported frameworks.

[Frameworks guide](/compliance/frameworks)

### Risk Register: expanded to align with NIST IR 8286

The risk register gained priority levels, per-category risk tolerance and an org-level risk appetite statement, gated risk acceptance (a written justification and a recorded approver — above-tolerance acceptance requires an Admin), gated closure (a closure rationale is now required), review-cadence tracking for overdue risks, rated control-link effectiveness, optional quantitative estimates, and a CSV export.

[Risk Register guide](/risk-trust/risk-register)

### Outbound webhooks and a write-back API

Settings → Webhooks lets you register endpoints that receive real-time events — ticket created, status changed, resolved, SLA breached, invoice generated, quote sent or decided, and more — signed so you can verify authenticity. A companion write-back API issues scoped API keys so external automation tools can post ticket notes and time entries back into Regentra, closing the automation loop.

[Webhooks guide](/settings/webhooks) · [API keys guide](/settings/api-keys)

### Ticket ↔ asset linking; new Settings → Tickets page

Technicians can now link assets directly to a ticket from the ticket side. A new Settings → Tickets page consolidates ticket-numbering and time-tracking billing settings in one place.

[Settings → Tickets guide](/settings/tickets)

### Scheduled monthly reports

A monthly digest email now sends automatically each month: an internal summary for your team, and a white-labeled, client-facing report per company.

[PSA reports guide](/psa/reports)

### Automation Value reporting

Automation rules now report the real, auditable work they've done instead of an unverifiable activity count, under a new Automation Value view.

[Automations guide](/psa/automations-and-ai)

### Acknowledgment campaigns: target by Entra security group; scheduled sends fixed

Campaigns can now target recipients by their Microsoft Entra ID group membership, in addition to individual selection. A bug that silently prevented non-immediate scheduled campaigns from ever sending has also been fixed.

[Acknowledgment campaigns guide](/compliance/acknowledgment-campaigns)

### Service Agreements: line items, contract attachments, company defaults

Agreements now has its own dedicated page with line items (monthly, annual, and one-time charge types), SOW/contract attachments, and the ability to set a company's default agreement so time entries auto-attach a rate even when none is chosen explicitly.

[Projects & agreements guide](/psa/projects-agreements)

### Security hardening: admin MFA mandate, simplified password policy

MFA is now mandatory for Admin, MSP Admin, and Super Admin roles regardless of your organization's MFA setting. Factor enrollment and removal both now require a freshly-verified factor from the same session. Password requirements simplified to a 12-character minimum with breach-database screening — no more forced character-composition rules. Trusted-device handling was also fixed to apply correctly across SSO logins and session resumes.

[MFA guide](/settings/mfa)

### Compliance dashboard drill-down; automatic control status changes

Dashboard tiles are now clickable drill-downs into the underlying controls and evidence, with an in-card search. Controls also now demote themselves from Implemented to Needs Review automatically on fresh failing monitoring evidence, and restore automatically once a fresh check passes.

[Compliance overview guide](/compliance/overview)

## 2026-05

### QuickBooks ↔ Stripe dual-system support

The most common MSP setup — using QuickBooks for books of record and Stripe as the payment processor — now works end-to-end automatically.

- **Stripe-paid → QuickBooks Payment recorded automatically.** When a customer pays an invoice through Stripe, Regentra creates a matching QB Payment so your QB AR aging report stays accurate. No more "phantom outstanding receivables" in QuickBooks.
- **QuickBooks-paid → Stripe invoice voided automatically.** If the customer pays through QB instead, Regentra voids the linked Stripe invoice so they can't double-pay.
- **Smart defaults.** When both Stripe and QB are connected, the QB Pay Now button defaults off so customers see one payment path. Toggleable per-MSP under Settings → Integrations → QuickBooks Mapping.
- **Primary-channel banner.** The invoice detail page now shows which channel is primary when both integrations are present.

[Full QuickBooks integration guide](/integrations/quickbooks)

### QuickBooks integration — full feature set

- **Auto-propagate Portal Company edits to QB** — name/phone/address changes flow to QB automatically.
- **Soft-delete QB customer when Portal Company is deleted** — no more orphaned QB customers.
- **Email invoice via QB** — trigger QB to email the customer the invoice from QB's own pipeline.
- **Mark Paid creates QB Payment** — manual mark-paid in Regentra now records a payment in QB ledger.
- **DRAFT invoice edit propagation** — notes and due-date changes propagate to QB.
- **Issue Credit Memo** — issue QB credit memos against PAID/VOID synced invoices from the invoice detail page.
- **Download QB-rendered invoice PDF** — pull the canonical QB PDF for support tickets and audit trails.
- **Auto-void linked Stripe invoice when QB-side payment lands** — closes the duplicate-charge window.
- **Webhook-health watermark** — the integrations card now shows "Webhook X ago" so admins can spot when Intuit stops reaching Regentra.
- **Reconnect banner** for connection issues — clearer prompt when a QB token expires.
- **International Pay Now gating** — non-US realms see ACH disabled automatically (Intuit rejects ACH for non-US).

### HIPAA Security & Privacy Officer designations

Per-framework officer designations independent of the role tier system. An admin can be the designated Privacy Officer for HIPAA without collapsing the two concepts. Per HHS guidance, the same person can hold both Security Officer and Privacy Officer slots in smaller covered entities — the schema models them separately so the audit record stays clean.

### Compliance setup gate relaxed

The post-consent compliance workspace now unlocks immediately after Microsoft Entra admin consent is granted. The first compliance sync runs in the background; a brand-color banner surfaces on compliance pages until the watermark lands so users know why asset/user counts are still empty. Replaces the previous behavior where users were stuck on the setup wizard until both consent and first sync completed.
