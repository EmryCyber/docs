What's shipped in Regentra, newest first. Each entry covers one week, or one month for earlier releases. Use the filters to show only the areas you care about.

Every entry starts with anything you need to do, or anything that now looks different, and then lists what's **New**, **Improved** and **Fixed**. For a single overview of a whole month, see the [September 2026 release notes](/releases/september-2026).

<Update label="September 30, 2026" description="Microsoft 365 calendar, invoice line items, product import, My account" tags={["PSA", "Billing", "Integrations", "Branding", "Account"]}>
  **What looks different**

  - Technicians' work notes are no longer printed on customer invoices. To show them, turn on **Settings → Tickets → Billing → Show work notes on invoices**.
  - Device owners are now worked out again on every sync from Intune, Level tags and the signed-in user. Some devices will show a different owner after the next sync. An owner a technician picked by hand is kept.
  - Billable time needs a product only once you map a product to a work type in **Settings → Tickets → Billing**.

  ### New

  - **Microsoft 365 calendar.** Technicians connect their own calendar in **My account → Calendar** and book customer appointments from a ticket, with a Teams meeting if they want one.
  - **Invoice line items.** Lines now have quantity, rate and an optional delivery period. The product picker is searchable, and favorite products are listed first. [Invoices guide](/psa/invoices-quotes)
  - **Edit time lines before generating an invoice.** Change the date, description, hours or rate of any line. The form explains time that was logged at $0. [Invoices guide](/psa/invoices-quotes)
  - **Product import and export.** Import products from a CSV file with a preview of every row, and export them again. Products can have an optional SKU. [Service catalog](/psa/service-catalog)
  - **My account.** Your profile, preferences, notifications, security and calendar settings are now in one place, separate from organization settings.
  - **@mentions in new tickets.** Mention a teammate in a new ticket's description and they're notified.
  - **Logo help.** Size guidelines, automatic trimming of empty margins, and a Canva template. Logos are larger on sign-in pages and PDFs. [Organization profile](/settings/organization)
  - **Company RMM cards.** The company page shows a card only for the RMM that company uses, adds a Level card, and shows when each sync last ran. [Companies & contacts](/psa/companies-contacts)

  ### Improved

  - Onboarding is now a guide, not a requirement. Nothing is blocked while it's unfinished.
  - New accounts verify their email before setting up a passkey, and the verification link works on any device. [MFA guide](/settings/mfa)
  - Picking a contact on a ticket links their device automatically. [Assets guide](/psa/assets)
  - Managed clients show their MSP's logo, colours and "Powered by" setting everywhere.
  - Sync from Stripe links products that already exist instead of adding a second copy, and reports any it skipped.
  - The compliance client switcher lists clients while you are switched into one.
  - The Teams tab's new-ticket form asks how urgent the issue is.
  - Getting Started now asks you to configure your support portal instead of adding a contact.
  - Colour fields have a fixed "#", so you only type the six digits.

  ### Fixed

  - Branded sign-in links no longer show "Internal Server Error".
  - Trial reminder emails go out again, and buying several modules together at checkout works.
  - The trial banner shows only real trials.
  - QuickBooks keeps charging sales tax when QuickBooks collects the payment.
  - A month's recurring agreement charge bills only what's still unbilled.
  - A device managed by both NinjaOne and Intune keeps its owner and compliance status.
  - Email attachments have longer to download. If they still fail, the ticket gets an internal note saying so.
  - Converting a live chat to a ticket works when retried, and Log Time won't save its default time window untouched.
  - An AI auto-response is never offered for sending a second time.
  - Bulk import and export rows in the activity feed open the right list.
  - Compliance admins and officers see security activity in the activity feed again.
  - Wide portal logos fit on phones.
</Update>

<Update label="September 26, 2026" description="Ticket time-entry workspace, email appearance, products on time, trial extensions, security fixes" tags={["PSA", "Billing", "Branding", "Security", "Account"]}>
  **What you need to do**

  - **Sender display name.** Microsoft 365 always uses the mailbox's own display name, so the **Sender Display Name** field is gone from Settings → Email. To change the name customers see, rename the mailbox in the Exchange admin center (**Recipients → Mailboxes → Display name**).

  **What looks different**

  - Connecting a Microsoft 365 tenant now has a second step: after granting admin consent, an administrator of that tenant signs in once more to prove it.
  - Ticket lists open on the whole team's tickets unless you or your organization chose a different default.
  - Organizations that haven't customized their email look now get Regentra's default navy and blue design.

  ### New

  - **Time-entry workspace on tickets.** An interactive timeline of the day, every technician's entries on the ticket, and **Save & add another**. Works on phones. [Time tracking guide](/psa/time-tracking)
  - **Products on time entries.** Each time entry becomes its own invoice line. Managers can bill approved weeks straight from Timesheets. [Time tracking guide](/psa/time-tracking)
  - **Email appearance.** Set the banner colour, button colour and logo background for customer emails, with a live preview and automatic readability checks. [Organization profile](/settings/organization)
  - **Trial extensions.** Support can add trial days, and a free week is offered when you cancel. [MSP billing](/msp/billing)
  - **Support portal logo background.** Choose system, white, black or transparent. [Support portal](/psa/portal)

  ### Improved

  - A priority a technician sets by hand is never changed by automation or AI. Priority is now edited from the ticket header.
  - Submitted time can be taken back to draft and corrected. Week status stays in step with individual entries.
  - The contacts list searches every contact, not just the first 500.
  - @mentions in ticket replies notify people, and saved responses appear in the reply editor.
  - Scheduled reports work for organizations that have only PSA.
  - Inbound email no longer loses messages on busy mailboxes, and failed messages are retried.
  - SLA timers stay correct across daylight saving time changes. [SLA policies](/psa/sla-policies)

  ### Fixed

  - Sign-in page buttons were invisible for some organizations.
  - Some Super Admin pages crashed.
  - Ticket links in emails now work when you're signed out and on custom portal domains.
  - Portal sign-in works for contacts whose email had capital letters.
  - Deactivating a contact saves immediately.
  - An agreement's monthly charge is billed once per month, and invoice lines always add up to the total.
  - Quick **Log time** no longer loses billable time once you use products.

  ### Security

  - Internal notes stay out of chat transcripts and AI reply drafts. Screenshots pasted into internal notes stay off the customer portal.
  - AI reply drafts only use the same customer's past tickets.
  - A Microsoft tenant can only be connected through proven admin consent.
  - Device syncs can no longer change or delete another client's assets.
  - A user or project with logged time, or a user who created password vault entries, can't be deleted. Deactivate them instead.
  - The activity feed shows each role only what it should see.
</Update>

<Update label="September 19, 2026" description="Ticket types, templates, checklists, custom fields, recurring tickets, calendar, data import, white-label" tags={["PSA", "Compliance", "Integrations", "Branding", "Security"]}>
  **What looks different**

  - Customers no longer pick a priority on the portal. They say how urgent the issue is, and your technicians set the final priority. A customer can no longer raise a ticket to Critical.
  - The Regentra bot in Teams now works only in one-to-one chats. In a channel or group chat it asks people to message it directly. For group conversations, use the [Teams Chat Bridge](/integrations/teams-chat-bridge). [Regentra bot](/integrations/regentra-bot)
  - Adding an existing organization as a managed client now sends an approval request to that organization's admins.

  ### New

  - **Ticket types, templates, checklists and custom fields.** Classify tickets by type, start from a template, and add checklists that can warn or block before resolving. Custom fields can appear on the customer portal. [Tickets guide](/psa/tickets) · [Ticket settings](/settings/tickets)
  - **Recurring tickets.** Create tickets automatically on a daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly schedule. [Ticket settings](/settings/tickets)
  - **Urgency × impact priority.** Customers say how urgent the issue is; technicians set the impact.
  - **Resolve and Close dialog.** Separate boxes for an internal resolution note and a message to the customer.
  - **Ticket list views.** Me, Unassigned or Team, with saved filters for the organization and for each person. Going back from a ticket returns to your filtered list.
  - **Tickets on projects.** Link tickets to project cards; cards show the time logged on their tickets. [Projects](/psa/projects-agreements)
  - **Calendar.** Schedule visits and appointments, get reminders, and subscribe from Outlook, Google or Apple.
  - **Getting Started checklist.** Progress comes from what's actually set up in your account.
  - **CSAT drill-down.** Click a satisfaction card on the dashboard to see every response. [Reports](/psa/reports)
  - **Data import.** Bring companies, service agreements, tickets and ticket history from another PSA. [Import & export](/settings/import-export)
  - **AI ticket search.** Describe a past ticket in plain words and find it.
  - **White-label sign-in and staff app.** Your own sign-in link, your branding in the app, and on every report and email. "Powered by Regentra" can be turned off. [Organization profile](/settings/organization)

  ### Improved

  - Timesheets list each technician's weeks, and managers can submit, approve or reject on a technician's behalf. [Time tracking guide](/psa/time-tracking)
  - Every role can view Evidence Collection, and Compliance Admins can set it up. [Evidence collection](/compliance/evidence-collection)
  - The Teams app and the company page were redesigned.
  - Ticket notification emails come from one engine, so nobody gets the same alert twice.
  - Hudu keeps your own edits, queues conflicts for review, and asks before switching to a different Hudu instance. [Hudu](/integrations/hudu)
  - Your own Microsoft tenant files your staff under your own company. [Microsoft Entra](/integrations/microsoft-entra)

  ### Security

  - Findings from a full security audit were fixed across sign-in, webhooks, file storage and integrations.
</Update>

<Update label="September 12, 2026" description="Integration lifecycle sync, asset telemetry, Entra evidence, Settings area" tags={["PSA", "Compliance", "Integrations", "Account"]}>
  ### New

  - **Integration renames and deletions.** Level, NinjaOne and Hudu renames and deletions are detected, with a review queue before company names change. [Level](/integrations/level-rmm) · [NinjaOne](/integrations/ninjaone)
  - **Customer name sync.** Name changes made in Stripe or QuickBooks flow back to the company. [Stripe](/integrations/stripe) · [QuickBooks](/integrations/quickbooks)
  - **Microsoft Entra lifecycle.** Users deleted in Entra are deactivated, and restored if they come back. Stale devices are marked inactive. [Microsoft Entra](/integrations/microsoft-entra)
  - **Asset details.** Last seen, logged-in user, security score, operating system and compliance state are now proper fields you can see and export. [Assets guide](/psa/assets)
  - **Entra evidence.** Ten new automated compliance checks using the permissions you've already granted. [Evidence collection](/compliance/evidence-collection)
  - **Time-entry presets and a rate card** by type of work. [Time tracking guide](/psa/time-tracking)
  - **Hudu integration** can be connected from Settings → Integrations. [Hudu](/integrations/hudu)

  ### Improved

  - Settings is its own area, separate from the PSA and Compliance apps.
  - A parent company's contact can be the primary contact for each of its locations. [Companies & contacts](/psa/companies-contacts)
  - Level alerts are re-checked every hour, so a missed alert notification no longer leaves a ticket missing or stuck open.
  - Integration setup guides match what the app actually does.

  ### Fixed

  - Organizations with only PSA are no longer sent to compliance client setup.
  - Buying PSA during a trial of both modules no longer affects Compliance access.
</Update>
