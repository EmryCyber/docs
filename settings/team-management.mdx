title: "Team Management"
description: "Invite team members, assign roles, manage seat limits, and control access across your organization."
---

Team management controls who has access to your Regentra organization and what they can do. Each team member is assigned a role that determines their permissions.

## Inviting team members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to team settings">
    Go to **Settings → Team Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Member">
    Enter the person's full name and email address, and select a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add & Send Welcome Email">
    The account is created immediately, and the new member receives a welcome email with a link to set their password and sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The set-password link in the welcome email expires after 72 hours. There's no in-app way to resend it once it expires — if a new member misses the window, an admin needs to trigger a password reset for them from the login page instead.
</Note>

## Roles

The Team UI displays user-friendly labels (left column below). The right column is the role identifier used by the API and audit logs.

| Display label | Role ID | Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | ADMIN | Full access to a single organization. Manages users, settings, integrations, and all modules. |
| **Compliance Admin** | COMPLIANCE_ADMIN | Full control over the compliance module. Adopts frameworks, manages controls, creates policies, generates reports. |
| **Approver** | COMPLIANCE_OFFICER | Reviews and approves policies, controls, and assessments. Updates control status and uploads evidence. |
| **Approver (Privacy)** | PRIVACY_OFFICER | Same scope as Approver, signaling a privacy specialization for data-subject requests and privacy impact assessments. |
| **Approver (Security)** | SECURITY_OFFICER | Same scope as Approver, signaling a security specialization for technical controls and incident response. |
| **Analyst** | EMPLOYEE | Does compliance work but cannot approve. Acknowledges policies, drafts evidence, runs assessments. |
| **Read Only** | READ_ONLY | View-only access to all modules. Cannot create, edit, or delete any data. Useful for auditors and executives. |
| **MSP Admin** | MSP_ADMIN | Manages the MSP relationship layer — creates client tenants, assigns technicians, oversees cross-tenant operations. |
| **MSP Technician** | MSP_TECHNICIAN | PSA-focused. Manages tickets, time tracking, assets, and the knowledge base. |

<Info>
  A user can hold only one role at a time. To change someone's role, go to **Settings → Team Members**, click on the user, and select a new role.
</Info>

## Compliance team designation

Mark specific team members as part of the **compliance team** to give them visibility in compliance workflows. Compliance team members appear as assignees for controls and risk assessments, and receive notifications for compliance-related events.

This designation is separate from roles — a Technician can also be on the compliance team if they need to handle both responsibilities.

## Seat limits and billing

Each active team member with PSA access counts against your plan's bundled seat allotment. The billing page shows your current usage against that allotment, and the **Monthly Total** reflects the actual Stripe charge — which is your bundled seat limit × per-seat price, not your current user count. See [Billing & Subscriptions](/msp/billing#per-seat-billing-psa) for the full breakdown.

When your active user count exceeds your bundled allotment, the Billing page shows an **Over plan limit** warning with an "After update" cost projection so you can budget the upgrade.

<Warning>
  You can still invite new members when you are over your bundled seat limit — Regentra does not block invites at the user-management layer. The Billing page will show the overage and the projected cost so you can upgrade your plan to match.
</Warning>

## Deactivating users

To remove a team member's access:

1. Go to **Settings → Team Members**
2. Click on the user you want to deactivate
3. Click **Deactivate**

Deactivated users immediately lose access. Their historical data (time entries, ticket replies, compliance actions) is preserved. Deactivating a user frees up one seat.

<Tip>
  Deactivation is reversible. You can reactivate a user from the same page to restore their access without losing any data.
</Tip>

## External auditor invitations

External auditors do not consume a team seat. Instead they get a
token-scoped invitation that opens their own portal at
`/audit/{token}` — no Regentra login, no access to your PSA module,
no cross-tenant visibility.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the auditor invites page">
    **Settings → Auditor Access → Invite Auditor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the auditor's details">
    - **Auditor Email** (required) and **Auditor Name** (optional)
    - **Expires In** — pick from 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, or 180 days, or 1 year
    - **Scope** — check which sections the auditor can see: Frameworks,
      Controls, Evidence, Policies, Risk Assessments, Vendors, Incidents,
      and Training
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    The auditor receives an email with a one-time link. On first
    click, they set a password and gain access to the portal.
    Subsequent visits go through a login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From the same page you can preview the auditor's portal yourself, copy
the invite link (in case you'd rather share it another way than the
automatic email), and revoke an active invitation — which immediately
invalidates the token. Each invite card shows its status, when it was
created and expires, and how many times and when the auditor has
accessed it.

The auditor portal exposes [Audit Requests](/compliance/audit-requests),
the sections you checked off as the invite's scope, an audit-
package ZIP download, and per-section CSV exports. The auditor cannot
write to your data — on a request they can only approve it or flag it
with a note back to you, leaving all other state-changing actions on
the customer side of the relationship.
