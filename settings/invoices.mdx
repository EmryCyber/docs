---
title: "Invoice Settings & Client Billing Preferences"
description: "Set invoice defaults, customer-facing billing details, PDF presentation and client review preferences."
---

Use **Settings → Invoices** for organization invoice defaults. On a company's page, use **Financial settings** for client-specific terms and presentation. These controls are separate from the accounting provider's own payment and tax configuration.

## Organization defaults

Set the billing email, payment-term defaults, invoice footer/instructions and invoice tax-ID visibility in the applicable invoice/company-profile settings. Invoice tax-ID visibility is independent from quote visibility. The billing email appears on native invoice presentation and supplies the release email's default Reply-To, before the support-email fallback. It does not grant permission to send from that address.

## Invoice email delivery

In **Settings → Invoices → Invoice email delivery**, choose the delivery owner used when approving and releasing reviewed invoices:

- **Regentra email service** uses the platform sender.
- **Microsoft 365 mailbox** uses an authorized connected mailbox. Leave the mailbox blank to use the connected default; Microsoft controls the From display name and Send As permissions.
- **Stripe** or **QuickBooks** sends through that provider, which must be an invoice destination. Its own sender, subject, PDF and reply settings apply.

Regentra and Microsoft 365 delivery support a Reply-To override, PDF attachment and available payment-link options. The subject accepts only `{msp_name}` and `{invoice_number}` placeholders; leave it blank for the default. Microsoft 365 also supports saving a copy in Sent Items.

The settings preview and review-batch approval show the resolved delivery details. Check them before release. Direct provider-send actions keep their provider workflow, and AP controls its own delivery. When syncing QuickBooks, disable its **Automatically send imported invoices** setting if it would send an additional copy.

For Microsoft 365, choose whether a missing connection stops delivery or uses Regentra instead. This fallback applies before an attempt when no connection is configured. Permission errors and uncertain delivery results stop for review; they do not silently switch senders. Investigate an uncertain attempt before sending another copy.

## Customer-facing details

A company's customer-facing billing name can differ from its internal organizational name. Configure its billing recipient/contact and structured address, then use Preview PDF to verify the Bill to block. Designated billing details are resolved consistently for saved PDFs, draft previews and approved native-email attachments.

Editing the billing name updates an already linked QuickBooks customer without replacing its customer identity. The first customer push to an accounting provider remains an explicit staff action; creating a company or completing Microsoft 365 onboarding does not create remote accounting customers automatically.

## Client financial settings

Enable the company's preferences before setting:

- Payment terms or a third-Monday due rule, plus optional PO and reference.
- **Review and release before sending or syncing**.
- **Email customer on release**, which supplies the initial batch preference; confirm the final choice before approval.
- Tax treatment, message/footer and PDF style.

Existing companies remain on their previous workflow until preferences are enabled. A specific invoice due date overrides a calculated rule. Payment terms and schedules do not turn on autopay.

## PDF layouts

Choose **Classic** (formerly **Service details**), **Modern**, **Minimal**, **Bold**, or the original **Standard** in **Settings → Invoices → Invoice design**. Standard defaults to A4; the other designs default to US Letter. You can choose A4 or Letter explicitly, set an accent colour, change the document/customer/items/amount-due labels, and show or hide quantity/rate columns and service periods. Use the sample preview before saving.

Client financial settings and the invoice form can select a particular layout or **Organization default**. The client's layout is copied onto a new invoice. Organization design controls are read whenever a PDF is rendered, so saving them also affects later downloads of older invoices that use those controls. An untouched organization retains its previous appearance.

Classic has boxed account/billing fields, detailed service lines and an itemized balance summary. Its message, client billing instructions and applicable Remit-To instructions print beside the totals; the organization's invoice footer supplies the closing text. The separate **Invoice page footer** card controls company name, phone, website and an optional text line. Page numbers print only on multi-page invoices.

Designs preserve stored totals and product headings. Long descriptions continue onto later pages. Provider-generated PDFs use their own layouts. An existing PDF uploaded to AP is not refreshed solely by a design change; use **Upload again** on the invoice when needed. See [Regentra PDFs in AP](/integrations/alternative-payments#use-regentras-pdf-in-ap).

Use [Preview PDF and review/release](/psa/invoices-quotes#saved-drafts-and-pdf-preview) to verify the final recipient, amounts and wording. Explicit tax lines print as tax; an unexplained difference is not silently labeled as tax. Confirm non-taxable treatment only for services that are actually non-taxable.
