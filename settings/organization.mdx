Organization settings control your Regentra account's global configuration. These settings apply across all modules — compliance, PSA, and the customer portal.

## Organization Profile

Go to **Settings → Company Profile** to manage everything about how your organization presents itself — on the app, and on every document your clients see. It's one consolidated card, grouped into six sections: **Identity**, **Address**, **Contact**, **Tax**, **Documents**, and **Attribution**.

<Tip>
  Everything in this card feeds the branding shown on quotes, invoices, PDFs, and emails your clients receive — see [Where this shows up](#where-this-shows-up) at the end of this section.
</Tip>

### Identity

- **Logo** — a landscape PNG (transparent background recommended) or JPEG, at least 600×200px, ideally 1200×400px, up to 2 MB. If you upload a WebP file, Regentra automatically generates a PNG version behind the scenes so it still renders correctly on PDFs.
- **Icon** (optional) — a separate square PNG or JPEG, 256×256px, used for compact headers where a wide logo doesn't fit.
- **Organization Name** — your display name, shown in the app and on documents.
- **Legal name** — only needed if it differs from your display name; falls back to the organization name if left blank.
- **Industry**
- **Brand color** — used as an accent color on the quote/invoice header preview and public-facing pages.

### Logo and sign-in previews

Use the logo uploader's size guidance, margin trimming and Canva template when preparing your artwork. Preview the portal, sign-in pages and PDF layouts rather than assuming the same wide image fits every surface. Email appearance also provides banner/button colors and logo-background controls with a readable preview.

A branded staff sign-in link keeps the chosen branding through supported password and verification pages. Staff sign-in belongs on the app's sign-in flow; the customer portal custom domain is a separate surface.

### Address

Address fields are structured: **Address line 1**, **Address line 2**, **City**, **State/Region**, **Postal code**, and **Country** (2-letter code). If your organization still has an address stored the old free-text way, a **Split legacy address** button appears once, to migrate it into these structured fields — after that, the structured fields are what's used everywhere.

### Contact

**Phone**, **Website**, **Support email**, and **Billing email**. Billing email is used as the reply-to address on invoice emails and the "questions?" line — if left blank, it falls back to your support email.

### Tax

- **Tax ID label** — defaults to "EIN," but you can change it to match what's appropriate for your country (there's no separate, fixed EIN field anymore — the label is just configurable text).
- **Tax ID** — the number itself.
- **Show tax ID on quotes** and **Show tax ID on invoices** are separate controls. Configure each document type deliberately.

### Documents (PSA only)

Defaults applied across your quotes and invoices:

- **Default payment terms** — how many days clients have to pay, used when generating a new invoice.
- **Invoice memo** — text shown above the totals on every invoice PDF.
- **Invoice footer**
- **Quote terms & conditions (default)**
- **Quote footer**
- **Remit-to instructions** — payment instructions (check payable-to, ACH/wire details) shown on the invoice PDF only.

<Note>
  These are organization-wide defaults. A quote can carry a per-document override for **who it's addressed to** (recipient name, email, phone, address), and client financial settings or an editable review invoice can override supported terms, message/footer and presentation. See [Invoice settings](/settings/invoices).
</Note>

### Attribution

**Show "Powered by Regentra"** — on by default. This one toggle controls a "Powered by Regentra" mark across every customer-facing surface: the public quote page, the quote PDF, quote emails, the invoice PDF, PSA client reports, and other public links. Turn it off for a fully white-labeled experience.

### Where this shows up

Everything above appears on: the public quote page and quote PDF, quote emails, the invoice PDF, PSA client report PDFs, and public token pages (e-signature, ticket, audit, vendor questionnaire, and shared-password links).

<Tip>
  Upload a high-resolution logo for the best appearance across every one of those surfaces.
</Tip>

## Timezone

Set your organization's timezone from the **Timezone & Display** card in **Settings**. This affects:

- SLA business hours calculation
- Report date ranges
- Scheduled sync times
- Dashboard date displays
- Date-range filters on [data exports](/settings/import-export)

<Note>
  SLA clocks record their hours configuration. Named-calendar and holiday edits can re-plan running clocks while preserving consumed time and breaches; do not assume every existing deadline is recreated simply from the current organization time zone. See [SLA calendars](/psa/sla-policies#business-calendars-and-holidays).
</Note>

## Custom domain for support portal

Configure a custom domain so your clients access the portal at your branded URL (e.g., `support.yourcompany.com`). See the [Customer Portal](/psa/portal) documentation for full setup instructions.

## Email configuration

Regentra sends email notifications for ticket updates, SLA alerts, and invoice delivery. Configure your email sending method from the **Email Integration** card in **Settings**:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Azure Communication Services">
    Use Azure Communication Services for email delivery. Enter your ACS connection string and verified sender domain.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Microsoft Graph">
    Send emails through a Microsoft 365 mailbox using Microsoft Graph API. Requires the Entra ID integration with mail send permissions.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Email notifications will not be sent until a valid email configuration is saved. Verify your setup by sending a test email from the settings page.
</Warning>

## Session duration

Go to **Settings → Authentication & SSO → Session Policy**. Choose the inactivity window and a maximum session lifetime of 4, 8 or 12 hours. Administrators also control remembered-device duration and whether browsers can remember accounts. Activity does not bypass the maximum lifetime.

Your own factors, trusted devices and active sessions are in **My account → Security**. See [Sessions and sign-in security](/settings/sessions).
